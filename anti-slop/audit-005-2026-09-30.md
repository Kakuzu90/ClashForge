## Findings

### 1. Toggle button hit target undersized (HIGH)
- **File:line:** `src/resources/js/Components/ui/UiSelect.vue:210–221`
- **Rule:** R-03 (mobile responsiveness, tap targets ≥ 44px)
- **Issue:** The toggle button for opening/closing the dropdown menu is 40px wide and lacks a `.hit-target` invisible 44×44 hit area. Per DESIGN.md owner decision (2026-09-30, "add hit area"), controls below 44px visual size must have an invisible `.hit-target` 44×44 wrapper for reliable touch targets on mobile.
- **Priority:** HIGH (Hard Gate rule)
- **Fix:** Wrap the button with a div `class="hit-target"` to expand the touch area to 44×44, keeping the visual 40px button centered inside.
