## Findings

### 1. Expand button touch target — AdminTable.vue:102–109

**File:** `src/resources/js/Components/admin/AdminTable.vue`  
**Line:** 102 (button element)  
**Rule:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness)  
**Priority:** MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**Reason:** The expand button for detail rows uses `size-10` (40×40 px), but R-03 and DESIGN.md owner decision require 44×44 touch targets. Per the decision (2026-09-30): "a `.hit-target` 44×44 invisible area covers touch" for controls sized below 44px. The button lacks an explicit hit-target area or adequate padding to reach 44×44.

**Fix:** Add `px-2 py-2` or equivalent padding to the button, or wrap it in a `.hit-target` 44×44 area. Visual size stays 40px; only the clickable area expands.
