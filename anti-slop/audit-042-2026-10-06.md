## Findings

### 1. GamePlayerCard link missing focus ring (HIGH)

**File:** `src/resources/js/Components/game/GamePlayerCard.vue:84`

**Rule:** R-32 (Hard Gate — Keyboard Accessibility)

**Reason:** Link has `outline-none focus-visible:underline` but spec 18 §8 requires "a visible 2px `--border-focus` ring and a 2px offset" on all interactive elements. Underline alone is insufficient; profile social links correctly use `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-focus`.

**Priority:** HIGH

**Fix:** Replace `class="outline-none after:absolute after:inset-0 focus-visible:underline"` with `class="outline-none after:absolute after:inset-0 focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-focus"` on the Link element.
