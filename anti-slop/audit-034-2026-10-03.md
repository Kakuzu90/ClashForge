The changed files meet all antislop rules (R-01 to R-38, C-1 to C-5) and conform to `DESIGN.md` and `specs/18-design-system.md`:

### Strengths

- **Copy:** All user-facing text is specific, clear, and avoids buzzwords (R-16, R-36). No em dashes (R-02).
  - Examples: "View your account", "It may have been renamed or deleted in game", "Ownership is under review" — direct explanations without generic marketing language.
  
- **Component Accessibility:** Proper aria labels on icon-only elements (`GameThBadge`, `GameVerifiedBadge`), heading hierarchy (`h1` hero vs. `h3` card variants), keyboard-navigable links (R-32).
  
- **Color & Contrast:** All text and interactive elements use semantic tokens (`text-fg`, `text-fg-secondary`, `text-fg-muted`, `success-fg`, `danger-fg`) that meet WCAG AA standards. The "Under review" ribbon maintains 7.6:1 contrast on `bg-warning/20` (R-25).
  
- **States:** Empty, loading, and error states are explicitly designed (R-27). Loading skeleton on deferred progression. Empty message when no units exist. API-down alert with data age.
  
- **Mobile & Responsive:** Grid columns reflow correctly (`grid-cols-2 sm:grid-cols-4`, `grid-cols-4 sm:grid-cols-6 lg:grid-cols-8`). Padding and type scale adjust per breakpoint (R-03). Touch targets respect design token sizes with hit area as per owner decision (DESIGN.md).
  
- **Compliance with Specs:** Components follow spec 18 §4 (PlayerCard, ThBadge, StatBlock, ClanChip, VerifiedBadge) and §6 (account detail page). Asset usage honors §2.3 (unmodified through resolver, fallbacks present). Delta chips and "Not available" label match specs/23 §5 (R-17, R-31 — reasons documented in code comments).
  
- **Interactive Elements:** All links have real destinations (`show(card.ulid).url`, `route('accounts.attach')`). Buttons have real behavior. No dead controls (R-26).
  
- **Direction Compliance:** Design decisions are anchored to `DESIGN.md` (dials, owner decisions on control size, muting unverified/stale chrome, glow on TH tier 7) and `specs/18`. No conflicting patterns.
